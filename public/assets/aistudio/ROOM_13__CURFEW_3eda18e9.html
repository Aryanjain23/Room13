How to play

  • Drag, scroll, use the arrow keys or the minimap to look around the room.
  • Tap anything with a glowing ring to inspect it.
  • Your objective and progress live at the top and bottom of the screen.
  • You get exactly two hints. A third is never available.
  • The timer builds suspense but never ends the game in Phase 1.
